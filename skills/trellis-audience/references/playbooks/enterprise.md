# Playbook: enterprise
Ladder: L3 discovery call kept with a named buyer · L4 signed letter of intent, paid pilot, or
design-partner agreement · L5 paid pilot delivering the outcome (often concierge-heavy).
A landing-page email test is the WRONG instrument; do not run L1–L2 as validation.
Channel map: founder channel = LinkedIn; rooms = private peer groups, industry events, analyst
communities, procurement-adjacent forums; warm introductions dominate.
Cold start: owner's and engineer's network first (honest assessment at A1); targeted outreach to
named roles; rented presence at vertical events; partner referrals.
Compliance: B2B legitimate interest with opt-out; respect procurement and security review timelines.
Benchmark sources: sparse and mostly private — pipeline conversion benchmarks from sales-ops
publications; design-partner programme write-ups. Expect to set unbenchmarked thresholds from the
economic floor and record them as such.
