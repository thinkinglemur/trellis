# Playbook: smb
Ladder: L2 email · L3 demo booked and kept / qualifying form · L4 pre-order, card on file, or signed
pilot at a nominal fee · L5 concierge paid delivery.
Channel map: founder channel = LinkedIn (usually); rooms = trade groups, Facebook/LinkedIn groups,
vertical Slack communities, local business networks; owned = newsletter; partners = tools the SMB already
uses (marketplaces/integrations).
Cold start: direct outreach (100 → 10–20 replies → 5–8 calls → 2–3 pilots); LinkedIn paid with
title/industry targeting as controlled sample; rented vertical newsletters; concierge.
Compliance: B2B outreach defensible on legitimate interest (UK GDPR/PECR corporate-subscriber rule)
with clear opt-out; sole traders are treated as individuals — no cold email.
Benchmark sources: SaaS landing/demo conversion reports (e.g. industry CRO benchmarks), outreach
reply-rate studies, pilot-to-paid conversion write-ups — always note sample and price-point context.
