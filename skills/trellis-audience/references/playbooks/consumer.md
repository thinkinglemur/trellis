# Playbook: consumer
Ladder: L1 click · L2 email · L3 waitlist with qualifying Qs / referral-to-advance · L4 refundable
pre-order or card on file · L5 concierge paid delivery.
Channel map: founder channel = where the actor is (short-form video, Reddit, niche forums); rooms =
subreddits, Discord, Facebook groups; owned = newsletter/waitlist; marketplaces = Product Hunt, app
stores. Referral/queue mechanics only if there is social value in being early.
Cold start: paid controlled sample (Meta/TikTok interest targeting) → L2 read in ~2 weeks; rented
audiences (creator shout-outs, niche newsletters); Product Hunt timing; concierge for L5.
Compliance: NO cold email to consumers (UK PECR / EU ePrivacy). Opt-in only. Double opt-in recommended.
Benchmark sources: Unbounce/HubSpot landing-page conversion reports by industry; Kickstarter/Indiegogo
category stats for L4; app-store conversion reports; published waitlist case write-ups — report
provenance and sample context with every number.
